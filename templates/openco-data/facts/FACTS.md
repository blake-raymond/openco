# Facts

This is the facts file. An agent may state a company claim only when it
appears here as an entry. Add new entries at the end. Do not edit an
entry after it has been committed.

A heading is `## YYYY-MM-DD - slug`. `category` is `claim`, `story`,
`number`, or `pricing`. Numbers and prices are sayable only as entries
in this file, even if a note about them also lives in the company brain.
